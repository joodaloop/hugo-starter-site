Coordinated research programs bring together researchers from academia, industry, and government under active program management to tackle ambitious technical challenges. Unlike traditional grants that fund independent research, coordinated programs actively manage a portfolio of projects, facilitate collaboration between teams, and dynamically adjust the research agenda based on progress and emerging opportunities.

DARPA programs exemplify this approach. Program managers with deep technical expertise define ambitious goals, select and fund research teams, facilitate information sharing between performers, and make real-time decisions about which approaches to scale up or discontinue. This active management enables rapid progress on complex challenges that no single team could address alone.

Coordinated research programs work best for ambitious, multifaceted technical challenges that require diverse expertise and benefit from active integration of results across teams. They require skilled program managers who combine technical depth with management ability—a scarce resource. When well-executed, coordinated programs can achieve breakthroughs faster than uncoordinated funding, but poor program management can waste resources and frustrate researchers.
